# Power BI Design Taste

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative design standard for Power BI reports in this repository.

The Visual Designer must follow this document when creating or modifying report pages, themes, visual formatting, navigation, spacing, hierarchy, and overall look and feel.

When an approved visual reference asset exists in this folder, use that asset together with this document.

Do not replace explicit project design direction with generic dashboard conventions.

## 15. Final visual acceptance checklist

Before handoff, confirm:

- Layout follows the approved grid.
- Typography follows the defined hierarchy.
- Colors come from the approved palette.
- KPI cards follow one consistent pattern.
- Visuals have intentional alignment and spacing.
- Filters and navigation behave consistently.
- Loading, blank and error states are accounted for.
- Accessibility expectations are met.
- The report does not use generic styling that conflicts with this document.
- Available approved references were inspected and applied.
